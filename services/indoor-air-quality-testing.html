Choose tests for the concern

A1 provides independent indoor air quality testing and consultation at homes, rental properties and workplaces. If an odor keeps returning or conditions changed after a leak, renovation or HVAC work, we can discuss an investigation suited to the property. You do not need to choose a testing package before calling.

Air testing for a home, rental or workplace

For a home purchase or renovation, the visit can focus on the indoor-air concern affecting your decision. Rental owners can discuss room-specific complaints and access through management. Businesses can identify occupied spaces, operating hours and recent changes. A1 confirms the scope and selected measurements or samples before the appointment.

Explore Greenville air quality testing, Greer air quality testing or Athens air quality testing for those properties. The full service-area directory covers our other listed communities.

Tell us when and where the concern occurs

Describe affected and unaffected rooms, when the issue began, and recent painting, cleaning, flooring, water or HVAC work. A short occurrence record can help when an odor is intermittent. A1 uses that history to plan the investigation and explain the conditions represented by the visit.

Discuss observations before selecting a sampling package

Depending on the question, the agreed scope may include moisture and humidity observations, ventilation context, or selected laboratory analyses. Ask what each proposed measurement can establish, which locations it represents, and what remains outside the scope.

Mold and moisture investigations, radon measurements, and asbestos material testing address different questions. Explain all concerns before the visit so they can be scoped separately where needed.

Connect findings to practical next steps

EPA describes source control, ventilation, and air cleaning as distinct approaches to improving indoor air quality. The useful next step depends on the source and the building. A test report should explain its findings and limits instead of treating a generic air-cleaning product as the answer to every concern.

EPA: approaches to improving indoor air quality

Plan access and operating conditions

Identify who can open locked rooms and supply maintenance records. For shared buildings, coordinate relevant areas with management. Explain how the property normally operates and record changes made before the visit so observations can be interpreted with that history.

Confirm the scope before booking

Environmental testing describes the conditions and scope assessed. Share building observations with A1; medical symptoms and individual health advice belong with your healthcare professional.

Testing and reporting schedule

Some observations can be discussed during the visit. Laboratory results are typically available within 48–72 business hours, depending on the selected samples and analysis. Confirm the complete reporting schedule before booking.
