Planning a Myrtle Beach renovation

For a Myrtle Beach lodging or condominium refurbishment, identify each room type and the surfaces included in the project. Similar-looking finishes in different units can have different replacement histories. Investigating moisture in Myrtle Beach

When a Myrtle Beach rental has a recurring odor between guest stays, preserve the timing and maintenance records before routine cleaning obscures the history. Note recent water incidents and how the HVAC system runs during vacancies. If several units are involved, their conditions should be documented individually rather than assumed to match the first room inspected.

For an indoor odor or other concern, explain where and when it occurs and what has changed at the property. We can discuss whether a mold assessment, asbestos inspection, or another indoor air quality testing scope fits the question. Location alone does not establish the presence of a contaminant.
