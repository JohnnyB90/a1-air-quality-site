Mold Testing & Inspection

Independent mold inspections, moisture assessments, and targeted sampling for homes and businesses across NC, SC, and Georgia service areas. Get a consultation.

100% Independent Testing — Zero Conflict of Interest. We never sell remediation or removal services. Our results and recommendations are always honest and unbiased.

Investigate the moisture problem and the mold concern together

A musty smell, a recurring leak, or a disputed cleanup can leave you unsure what to do next. A1 provides independent mold inspections and targeted sampling to investigate the concern and document findings. We do not sell mold removal or remediation.

Testing is not automatically necessary whenever mold is visible. EPA explains that visible growth can often be addressed without sampling. An inspection can help clarify the moisture source, the extent of the concern, and whether sampling would answer a useful question. Read EPA guidance on when mold sampling is useful.

When an independent assessment can help

  • Persistent musty odors or suspected hidden moisture damage.
  • Water intrusion, leaks, or humidity problems that need investigation.
  • A property purchase or tenant concern requiring documented findings.
  • Questions about conditions after a remediation project, assessed against an agreed scope.

What mold samples can and cannot tell you

Air samples describe airborne spores captured during a particular sampling period. Surface samples investigate a specific sampled location. Neither method finds every hidden growth area, and identification is limited by the analytical method used.

There are no federal limits for mold or mold spores. Interpretation should consider the inspection, moisture, sample locations, indoor and outdoor conditions where relevant, and the laboratory method. A universal indoor/outdoor ratio or the presence of a single spore is not a medical diagnosis or a reliable stand-alone declaration that a building is safe or unsafe.

Color alone does not establish a mold's identity or health risk. If you have health concerns, discuss them with a healthcare professional; a building inspection does not diagnose the cause of symptoms.

Your report and next steps

Before booking, confirm the areas to be inspected, proposed samples, included laboratory analysis, and written report. The report should explain the observed conditions, sampled locations, results, and limits of the assessment. If remediation is appropriate, the findings can help you discuss the work with a separate contractor.
